# Paras campaign rules

The AI applies one of two templates per prospect. It sends only when it finds a specific, factual detail from the supplied website, notes, or approved social data.

## Template 1 — website / social detail

**Subject:** A few Meta Ads ideas for {{business_name}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I was looking at {{business_name}} and really liked {{specific_verified_detail}}.

I’m Paras, a Meta Ads and lead-generation specialist based in India. Before working independently, I was part of the Sales & Marketing team at Supremex India, where I worked on a Meta campaign for the automotive protection industry.

The campaign generated **186 leads from ₹16,157.82 in ad spend**, averaging **₹86.87 per lead**. The case study is attached only when configured.

Would {{meeting_option_1}} or {{meeting_option_2}} work for a 10–15 minute meeting?

## Template 2 — verified PPF/detailing business

**Subject:** More PPF leads for {{business_name}}?

Use only when automotive protection/detailing and the city are verified. Ask whether the recipient wants 2–3 specific ideas. Do not fabricate the city, services, or opportunities.
